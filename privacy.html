Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 27 Sept 2025

Daily Motivation App (“we,” “our,” or “us”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to log in. However, we automatically generate a unique user ID for each user to personalize your experience. We may collect basic interaction data such as likes, bookmarks, and selected categories to improve your app experience. No personally identifiable information is collected.

2. How We Use Information

The information we collect is used solely for:

  • Providing personalized quotes
  • Managing bookmarks and likes
  • Serving relevant content categories
  • Improving app functionality

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Some anonymous data may be used by Google AdMob to serve relevant ads.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob for advertisements. Please refer to Google’s privacy policy for more details on how your data may be processed.

5. Your Choices

You may manage categories, likes, and bookmarks at any time within the app. If you wish to stop using the app, you can uninstall it from your device.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: app.support+dailymotivationalapp@giaro.site
