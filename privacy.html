Privacy Policy – Daily Motivation App

Effective Date: 27 Sept 2025

Daily Motivation App (“we,” “our,” “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information while using the Daily Motivation App (“App”).

1. Information We Collect

  • Automatically Generated User ID: We assign an anonymous user ID to identify your preferences and app usage. No personal login or personal information is required.
  • App Usage Data: Your interactions with the app, such as liked quotes, bookmarked quotes, and selected categories.
  • Device Information: Device type, OS, and app version to improve functionality.
  • Advertisements: Google AdMob may collect information for personalized ads, including device identifiers, IP addresses, and app interactions.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Personalize your experience with quotes and categories.
  • Allow bookmarking and liking via your anonymous user ID.
  • Display personalized ads through Google AdMob.
  • Improve app functionality and performance.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.
  • Google AdMob may collect and use information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Security

We implement reasonable measures to protect your information, but no app or service can guarantee 100% security.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Choices

  • Disable personalized ads via Google ad settings.
  • Clear app cache to remove locally stored preferences.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted in the app with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us
